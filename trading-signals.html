      Frequently Asked Questions

      How does the Breakout Scanner work?

      The Breakout Scanner automatically analyzes the daily charts of the top 80 Indian stocks (Nifty 50 + Broad Market) to find explosive momentum setups. It uses a separate engine that ignores standard mean-reversion metrics. Instead, it looks for a Volatility Squeeze (price consolidating tightly within Bollinger Bands) followed by a massive Volume Surge (150%+ above average trading volume) that pushes the price into a new breakout trend. It also penalizes setups if the overall Nifty 50 index is currently bearish.

      How are the trading signals calculated?

      Our engine uses a multi-factor composite scoring system. It evaluates RSI, MACD, EMA/SMA trend alignment, Bollinger Bands, and volume confirmation. It also calculates ATR-based risk context (volatility and illustrative stop zones). Each factor contributes to a total score between -6.5 and +6.5.

      What does a "Strong Bullish" or "Strong Bearish" setup mean?

      Unlike basic screeners that trigger on a single indicator, a Strong setup on GetNiftyReady requires a high magnitude score plus confidence and trend/momentum confirmation. This filters out market noise and reduces one-indicator false signals.

      Is this financial advice?

      No. Our signals are mathematically generated based on trailing, historical price data. They are designed to assist day-traders and swing-traders in identifying technical momentum, but they do not account for fundamental news, earnings reports, or broader macroeconomic factors. Always consult a SEBI-registered advisor before executing trades.
