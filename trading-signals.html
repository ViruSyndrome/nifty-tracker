      Frequently Asked Questions

      How are the trading signals calculated?

      Our engine uses a multi-factor composite scoring system. It evaluates the Relative Strength Index (RSI) for overbought/oversold conditions, MACD for momentum crossovers, 20-day and 50-day Simple Moving Averages (SMA) for trend alignment, and Bollinger Bands for volatility. Each indicator contributes to a total score between -6.5 and +6.5.

      What does a "Strong Bullish" or "Strong Bearish" setup mean?

      Unlike basic screeners that trigger on a single indicator, a Strong setup on GetNiftyReady requires at least 75% consensus across all four technical indicators, alongside a high magnitude score. This filters out market noise and highlights high-probability technical setups.

      Is this financial advice?

      No. Our signals are mathematically generated based on trailing, historical price data. They are designed to assist day-traders and swing-traders in identifying technical momentum, but they do not account for fundamental news, earnings reports, or broader macroeconomic factors. Always consult a SEBI-registered advisor before executing trades.
